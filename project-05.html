Funeral Of The Rose

Architecture + Interior Design
Breakup Memorial Tower
2022

Breakups can cause a lot of negative emotions and effects. I experienced breakup last year and couldn't get out of it. Therefore, I was thinking whether there could be an architecture to help us getting out of this hurt?

The concept “bond” is created due to the love between two people, which was used to build the main structure of the building. Also, the concept of giving up in love was applied in memorial capsule to store the history of love. I referred to the process of cemetery construction and designed different types of spaces to memorize this process. The building is designed and constructed by visitors, which also symbolized the definition of love.
