Garbage, don't cry

Recycled Material Installation
Reclaimed fruit crates, cardboard boxes
50 × 30 × 25 cm | 2022

I found this fruit shelf in the garbage, and it looked like it was crying when I found it. The fruit rack has always been an attachment to the fruit, protecting and supporting the fruit. When I tried to support the shelf with posts, new routes were formed. I received this inspiration and decided to transform it into marble run. Here the fruit rack does not need to be hidden to protect others, it is its own protagonist.
